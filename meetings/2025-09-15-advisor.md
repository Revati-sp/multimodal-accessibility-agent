## 1) Accomplished Since Last Meeting

- The team reviewed existing research systems and commercial assistants for autonomous digital-task execution.
- We identified that several individual actions in our original examples, such as sending messages, sending email, and scheduling, are already supported by Siri, Gemini, and other assistants.

## 2) Accomplished During This Meeting

- We discussed that the project should not claim simple assistant actions as its main contribution.
- The professor directed us to study NemotronLabs VoiceChat, interruption handling, and full-duplex behavior.
- We were also directed to study local language models running on phones and other devices.
- The project direction was refined from an assistant that performs common commands to an architecture and evaluation study of reliable, interruptible, and privacy-aware orchestration across local models, cloud models, APIs, tools, and GUI interaction.



## 3) Issues / Blockers

- We needed to identify a research contribution that was not already fully implemented by existing commercial assistants.
- We needed to understand how local models handle limited memory, computing resources, battery use, privacy, latency, and tool access.
- We needed to study how an agent responds when a user interrupts, corrects, or stops an ongoing task.
